錄音筆

錄音筆是一款簡單、直覺的 iOS 錄音工具，適合快速記下課堂重點、會議內容、靈感備忘與日常聲音紀錄。

Recorder is a simple iOS voice recording app for capturing notes, meetings, ideas, and everyday audio moments.

錄音內容會儲存在你的裝置中。你可以自行播放、重新命名、刪除或透過 iOS 分享面板匯出錄音檔。

Your recordings are stored on your device. You can play, rename, delete, or export selected files through the iOS share sheet.
