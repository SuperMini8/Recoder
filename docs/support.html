如何使用 / How to Use

  • 點選錄音按鈕開始錄音；第一次使用時，系統會要求麥克風權限。 Tap the record button to start recording. The system asks for microphone permission the first time you record.
  • 錄音時可暫停，完成後點選停止，錄音會儲存在裝置中。 You can pause while recording. Tap stop to save the recording on your device.
  • 可在錄音前選擇低音質、中音質或高音質。 Choose low, medium, or high quality before recording.
  • 點選列表中的錄音檔後，可以播放、暫停，或拖曳進度條調整播放位置。 Select a recording from the list to play, pause, or drag the slider to change playback position.
  • 選取錄音後可重新命名，或使用分享按鈕匯出錄音檔。 After selecting a recording, you can rename it or export the audio file with the share button.
  • 進入編輯模式後，可刪除不需要的錄音。 Enter edit mode to delete recordings you no longer need.

常見問題 / FAQ

  • 無法錄音時，請確認 iOS「設定」中的麥克風權限是否已開啟。 If recording does not start, check that microphone permission is enabled in iOS Settings.
  • 錄音檔無法播放時，請確認該檔案仍存在，並嘗試重新開啟 App。 If a recording cannot be played, make sure the file still exists and try reopening the app.
  • 分享功能會使用 iOS 系統分享面板；可分享的目的地取決於你的裝置與已安裝的 App。 Sharing uses the iOS share sheet. Available destinations depend on your device and installed apps.
  • 刪除錄音後無法在 App 內還原，請在刪除前確認是否已備份或分享。 Deleted recordings cannot be restored inside the app. Please back up or share important files before deleting them.
